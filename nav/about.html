About me

I'm Antonio Zafitombo, a Malagasy developer with a degree in Computer Science and Computer Engineering earned in 2025 at Tomsk State University of Control Systems and Radioelectronics, Russia. My background combines Python programming, web development, database design and modelisation.

From web platforms and personnel databases to solar-panel data collection and hydroxyapatite hardness modeling, my projects reflect one ambition: turning complex challenges into useful digital solutions.

I'm working toward becoming a full-stack web developer and building a technology company in Madagascar — one that serves local and international needs while creating opportunities for young Malagasy talent.
